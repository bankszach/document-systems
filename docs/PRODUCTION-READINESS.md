# Production readiness claim withdrawn

The July 28, 2026 production-readiness conclusion was withdrawn on July 29,
2026 after the World Control Plane evaluation exposed a consequential defect
that Document Systems did not detect.

The historical engineering measurements remain available in
[Experimental Evaluation v0.3](EXPERIMENTAL-EVALUATION.md), but they must not be
interpreted as product fitness, superior review quality, or production
readiness.
